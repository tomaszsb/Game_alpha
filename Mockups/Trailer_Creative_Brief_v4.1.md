## Purpose & Goals
### Primary Objective
Create a **50–55 second trailer** that converts curiosity into playtests by:
1. Selling the **challenge** first.
2. Highlighting tension, consequences, and the unique shared‑screen + phone setup.
3. Ending with a frictionless CTA: **game.unravelcodes.com/challenge**.

### Secondary Objectives
- Hook viewers in **3 seconds**.
- Show real gameplay by **8 seconds**.
- Show 3+ players holding phones by **15 seconds**.
- Use emotion over explanation.
- Deliver 60s, 30s, 15s, and vertical cuts.

---

## Narrative Spine (Emotional Arc)
1. **Conflict** — NYC denies you.  
   *“NYC said no.”*
2. **Chaos** — disasters, consequences, blame.  
   *“Every choice. Every consequence.”*
3. **Triumph** — Certificate of Occupancy moment.  
   *“Or… beat the system.”*
4. **Challenge** — FOMO + CTA.  
   *“90% of players fail. Will you?”*

## Shot Recipes (For Repeatability)
### Phone Reaction Close‑Up
- 50mm lens, vertical framing.
- 0.5s buzz → tap → red DENIED flash.

### Disaster Cutaway
- 0.3s SFX hit.
- 0.7s collapse animation.
- 0.5s player reaction.

### CO Win Moment
- 1s CO hold.
- 0.5s confetti.
- 1s group cheer.

---

## Vertical Composition Rules (9:16)
- Phones in **bottom 40%**.
- TV screen in **top 40%**.
- Captions in **center 20%**.
- Avoid top 20% (notch zone).
- Avoid bottom 20% (gesture zone).

---

## CTA Optimization
### CTA Variants for A/B Testing
- **“Scan to play free.”**
- **“Scan to beat NYC.”**
- **“Scan now — no download.”**

### QR Best Practices
- Minimum **500px** square.
- 10% padding.
- Black on white only.
- Add micro‑caption: **“Works on any phone.”**

---

## Sound Mixing Rules
- SFX **10–15% louder** during disasters.
- Music dips **20%** during captions.
- Phone buzz: boost low frequencies.
- Permit stamp: metallic, sharp.

---

## Color & Lighting Direction
### Color Palette
- **Primary:** NYC steel blue (#2C3E50), concrete gray (#7F8C8D).
- **Accent:** Permit red (#E74C3C), budget green (#2ECC71), caution yellow (#F1C40F).

### Lighting
- Cool (4500K) for tension.
- Warm (5200K) for success.
