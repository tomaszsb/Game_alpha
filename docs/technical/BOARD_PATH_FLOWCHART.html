The board path: Hire a Builder to the final sign-off

Every box is a space (the name players see). Every arrow is a choice or a dice outcome with its chance out of 6, the days and the dollars. All numbers are read from the game's data files by scripts/build-board-flowchart.mjs.

What the data shows (read from the same files)

  • Every path from Hire a Builder can reach Finish (checked by following the data).
  • No dead ends: every box has a way out.
  • Odd data: in DICE_EFFECTS the "time" row for The Audit and Fire Dept. Review holds place names instead of days (a copy of the Next Step row). The days there come from the fixed "Spend N days" row, so nothing breaks, but the button says "See how long it takes" while the roll only decides where you go.
  • No quality points are set on any box in this stretch. Quality changes only through cards (life events and the like), which are not drawn here.
  • The builder offers (how many, the price and time multipliers, the quality points) come from TROPHIES.csv, not from the space tables, so they are in the Hire a Builder box but have no arrows of their own.
  • Hire a Builder (first visit) lets you go on to the Special Inspector OR back to Lender Review (MOVEMENT.csv): the way back to the lender is the only way to re-open funding after choosing a builder.
  • Final Approval is not symmetric: roll 6 sends you to Fire Dept. Intake on a first visit but to Site Inspection on a return visit, and finishing is 4 in 6 first time but 5 in 6 on a return.
  • Fix It First costs 5 days and $10000 fees for sure, and still sends you back to Trouble On Site 1 in 6 (the same chance as Steer the Inspector's loop-back is 2 in 6).
  • Audit and Fire Dept. Review push-backs: with the expeditor card the push-back costs 0 days (spends the card); without it the days in the box apply. The approval rolls (which of 1 to 6 means approved) are decided in ApprovalService.ts and agree with the dice table; the Fire Dept. Intake desk uses a question chain (LOGIC_QUESTIONS.csv), not dice, so it has no chances.
