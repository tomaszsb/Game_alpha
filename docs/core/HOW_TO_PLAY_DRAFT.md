**Title:** How to play

**Your job**
You are the project manager on a building in New York City. Get the plans approved, the money lined up and a
builder hired, then finish the job. Every step costs days and dollars, so watch both.

**Each turn**
1. Read what the person at your space is telling you.
2. Do what it asks: hire a helper, pick a loan, answer a question. A space with something to do waits for you.
3. When you are done, press and hold End Turn to move on. A quick tap shows what moving on will cost.

**What you are juggling**
- Work: the jobs you take on, and how big the project is (its scope).
- Bank loans and investors: money you borrow or raise to pay for it.
- Expeditors: people who file your paperwork faster, or slower if you hire the wrong one.
- Life events: real-world surprises like new laws or weather.

**The road**
Owner: set the scope and find the money. Architect and engineer: draw it up. The city (DOB and FDNY): review and
approve. Builder: hire one and build. Each stop tells you what it needs.

**Not happy with an offer?**
You can push back and ask again. The first answer comes back to you, but it costs a small fee and five more days.
Each push costs again, so check the price before you press.

**How you win**
Three prizes are in play: Fastest, On budget and Best built. Win two of the three and the project is yours.
If nobody wins two, the lowest total of the three decides. You only need to reach the end to finish.

**How you lose**
You run out of money, your design fees pass 20% of the project's scope, or the job runs so long the owner
pulls the plug.
