## Current sprint
**Onboarding Phase C — teaching a beginner the game instead of testing whether they already know it.** v3.2.50 named the 27 board tiles, v3.2.51 authored 81 button labels, v3.2.52 fixed the places those labels never reached, v3.2.53 fixed the commit spine demanding an action that could not unblock it — and **v3.2.54 starts the teaching layer proper.** Every action row now carries a **"What’s this?"** disclosure as a SIBLING of the action button (never a child — `TextWithTerms` renders a term as `<span role="button">` with `stopPropagation()`, so a term inside a real `<button>` swallows the press). That is the only place a hard word can define itself, which is why v3.2.51’s plain buttons taught a beginner what to press and nothing about what it meant. It connects what already existed and reached nobody: 44 authored `ACTION_TOOLTIPS.csv` rows, a `TooltipService` loaded at startup and read by nothing, and `getManualEffectTooltip` with zero call sites. Two defects surfaced building it — the glossary had **never highlighted a plural** (‘Expeditor’ could not match “Expeditors”; 25 real plurals in the shipped copy now link), and `GLOSSARY.csv` row 244 was **the header line pasted back into the data**, making the word “term” open a definition reading “definition”. **Still open, and the bulk of it: the tutorial, the micro-lessons, and a voice pass on the 44 tooltip rows** — they predate the 2026-09-03 beginners fork and still read like it.

v3.2.53 came off the 2026-09-06/09-07 robot playtests. On every choice-movement space the reason line under the commit button named the wrong gate: at `PM-DECISION-CHECK` it read *Finish “Swap one helper for another” above first* while the only outstanding requirement was picking a destination. `replace_e` is **skippable** — `calculateRequiredActions` deliberately leaves `replace_/return_/give_` out of `requiredActions` — so the named action could never clear the gate. Underneath it, `‘Pick where you’re going first’` was **unreachable dead code** on all 12 choice-movement spaces, because `remaining > 0` always won the branch race. The fault predates v3.2.52, which only turned a vague wrong instruction into a specific one. Root cause of the omission: the skippable rule was hand-written in three files and missed in a fourth; all four now call `isSkippableEffectAction`. **What the playtest data does NOT establish:** the deploy and a Jarvis agent-runtime update (2026-09-05 04:30, `PATCHES BROKEN`) sit in the same window, so the collapse from 80-step games to 5–22 cannot be attributed to either. The defect was proven from the code and a seeded test failure, not from the bot.

v3.2.52 came off the 2026-09-05 robot playtest and its root cause was v3.2.51's own. `collapsePairedDiceActions` merges two dice rows that share one physical roll into a single button — and on merging, **overwrites the authored label** with a hardcoded `COLLAPSED_DICE_LABEL`. On 8 space/visit combinations, so **16 of the 46 authored dice labels never reached a player**, and the bureaucrat wording the maintainer's rule forbids survived in the one slot the CSV guard structurally cannot see. Same family as v3.2.51's own generator bug, one stage later: there the generator wrote the wrong column, here the renderer overwrites the right one.

Two more of v3.2.51's labels were fixed on their own merits: "Cut back your help" (the report's #1 confusion at 57 hits) never said it costs you *one* helper or that they are gone. Now "Let one helper go" / "Swap one helper for another", maintainer's wording.

**Still open in Phase C, and the bulk of it: the tutorial, tooltips and micro-lessons.** Tiles, buttons and now label delivery were all the mechanical half.
